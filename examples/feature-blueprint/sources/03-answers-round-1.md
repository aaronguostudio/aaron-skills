# S3 · Clarification round 1 · 2026-10-03

**Scripted fictional product-owner answers, not Aaron's or a real stakeholder's approval.**

Q1. Who may review? Only the studio coordinator; no self-approval or self-rejection.
Requesters see their own records. The coordinator sees all submitted and decided
requests, but not another person's drafts. A requester cannot review a request.
This replaces the meeting suggestion that anyone could approve.

Q2. When may a requester edit? Only their own draft. Submitted requests are locked.
A rejection includes a reason; the owner can revise it into a draft and submit
again using the same request ID. Approved requests are terminal in this pilot.
This replaces the suggestion to edit after sending.

Q3. What does approval mean? Permission to borrow, not a stock reservation. No
availability guarantee, conflict detection, checkout or returns in the pilot.
Equipment is one of Camera kit, Lighting kit or Audio kit. Submit requires a
10–240 character purpose and valid start/end dates, end on or after start. Drafts
may be incomplete. Dates are calendar dates, with no timezone conversion or
past-date rule in v0. New requests preselect Camera kit.
