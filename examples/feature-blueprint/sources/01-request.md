# S1 · Initial request · 2026-10-01

**Authored fictional fixture.** Fieldnote Studio, every person, requirement and
meeting in this example is invented. No customer project or real meeting was used.

We lend camera equipment to teammates. Requests get lost in chat. Give teammates
one place to ask for a kit and let the studio coordinator approve it. Show the
requester what happened. We want a small internal pilot, not an inventory system.

A request should include equipment, dates and a reason. We have camera, lighting
and audio kits. Dates refer to whole calendar days at the studio. We do not need
real names or emails in this design exercise.
