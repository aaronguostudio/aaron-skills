Show workshop registration status

Show participants whether their request is pending, confirmed or cancelled. Keep coordinator confirmation so people know when they can rely on a seat.

RequirementsOperating modelPrototypeData modelNext steps

Requirements

  • Show Pending after submission so the participant knows a seat is not reserved yet.
  • Let the coordinator confirm when capacity is available, so the participant can rely on the place.
  • Allow cancellation; release a seat only when a confirmed registration is cancelled.

Operating model

  • Who: participants submit and cancel; the coordinator confirms places.
  • When: status changes after submission, confirmation or cancellation.
  • What follows: confirmed participants can plan to attend; a pending request still needs a decision. Full sessions leave requests pending.
